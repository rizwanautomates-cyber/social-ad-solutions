Privacy Policy

Your information, handled with care.

This policy explains, in plain language, what information Social Ad Solutions collects through this website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to share with us when you enquire about our services:

  • Contact details you provide through the enquiry form, WhatsApp, email or our on-site chat — such as your name, business name, email address and WhatsApp number.
  • The details of your enquiry or project that you describe to us.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when visiting any website, such as device and browser type, handled by our hosting provider.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share to respond to your enquiry, discuss and provide our services, prepare proposals, and communicate with you about work we are doing together. We do not sell your personal information.

Communication & Enquiries

Our enquiry form opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled; nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Conversations on WhatsApp, email and social platforms are governed by those platforms' own privacy policies as well as this one.

Data Protection

We take reasonable care to keep enquiry information private and accessible only to Social Ad Solutions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security — please avoid sending sensitive personal or patient information through enquiry channels.

Third-Party Services

This website is hosted on Vercel and links to third-party platforms including Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and WhatsApp. The on-site AI assistant, when active, processes chat messages through Google's Gemini service to generate replies. These providers process data under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Cookies & Analytics

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies of its own. Third-party services loaded by the site — such as font and script providers, or the platforms you visit through our links — may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Information Sharing

We do not share your enquiry information with third parties for their marketing. Information is shared only where needed to respond to you, to provide a service you have requested, or where required by law.

User Rights

You may ask us at any time what personal information you have shared with us, and request that we correct or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The current version will always be the one published on this page.
